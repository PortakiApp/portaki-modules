# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

First stable release. The access guide tells a guest exactly how to get in, and keeps the codes hidden until the host says so.

* Guests see the entry method, building access, parking and the route to the door, step by step, with a map and an optional video.
* Access codes stay hidden until the moment the host picks: from booking, 24 h before, the day before at 4 pm, or at check-in.
* The host fills one Access tab on the property — entry method and codes, building access, parking, arrival route.
* When the Nuki module supplies a smart lock, a button in the booklet opens the door.
