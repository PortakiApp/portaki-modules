# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

First stable release. The end of the stay is where reviews are won; this module asks for one while the guest is still in the room.

* At the end of the stay, guests get a thank-you message and a way to leave a review.
* Reviews go to Airbnb through a button and a QR code, or stay in the booklet as a star rating with a comment.
* The host picks where reviews are collected, and is emailed for every rating left in the booklet.
