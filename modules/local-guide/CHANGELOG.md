# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

First stable release. The host's own short list of good addresses, on a map, instead of a generic city guide.

* Guests see up to six places picked by the host, each with a category, a distance and a description, on a map.
* When the host turns them on, bookable activities and tickets nearby round out the list, with photo, price and rating.
* The host enters places, pins them on the map and writes the disclaimer shown under the list.
* The tickets and activities catalogue comes from Portaki; both sections are off by default.
