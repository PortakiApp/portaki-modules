## 1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

First stable release. The network name and password, shown at the right moment, instead of a photo of the router label.

* Guests find the network name and password in the booklet, with a tip and the steps to connect.
* The password appears on the host's schedule: from booking, 24 h before, the day before at 4 pm, or at check-in.
* The host fills a Wi-Fi sheet — network name, password, tip and connection steps.
