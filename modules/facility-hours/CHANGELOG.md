# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

First stable release. Pool, spa, gym: when each one is open, answered before the question is asked.

* Guests check the opening hours of the pool, spa, gym or any shared facility without asking.
* Each facility carries its hours, its details and its own note, plus one general note.
* The host enters up to six facilities from an Hours tab.
