## 1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

First stable release. Bookings arrive in Portaki on their own, imported from the calendars the host already keeps.

* Stays are created and then kept up to date automatically from the host's own platform calendars.
* The host pastes .ics export links from Airbnb, Booking.com, Abritel/Vrbo, Google Calendar or any other calendar.
* A card shows sync history, and an email flags an unreachable calendar or new stays coming in.
* Nothing appears in the guest booklet; sync frequency depends on your Portaki plan.
