## 1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

First stable release. The numbers a guest needs in a bad moment, in the booklet rather than in a text thread.

* Guests find the useful numbers the host chose — pharmacy, doctor, repairs — and a reminder of the 112 emergency number.
* The host can show their own phone line, so a guest always knows who to call.
* One Emergency tab holds your number and up to six contacts, each a label and a number.
